# Executive KPI Scope

## Project

Digital Commerce Performance Analytics

## Phase

Phase 7 — Executive KPI Layer

## Work Package

P7A — Executive KPI Scope

## Purpose

Define the approved executive KPI set, decision use cases, reporting perspectives, upstream dependencies, metric ownership, and semantic boundaries for the Executive KPI Layer.

Phase 7 does not redefine business metrics already governed by the Phase 6 KPI contracts.

Its purpose is to organize governed business-mart outputs into a controlled executive analytical layer that supports performance monitoring, trend analysis, comparison, and driver analysis without duplicating upstream business logic.

---

# 1. Executive Consumer

The primary consumer is a business or ecommerce leader who needs a concise view of overall digital-commerce performance.

The Executive KPI Layer must support questions such as:

- How much purchase revenue is being generated?
- How much transaction activity is occurring?
- How much traffic is the digital-commerce experience receiving?
- How many sessions generate at least one purchase?
- How efficiently are sessions converting?
- What is the average value of a transaction?
- How much commercial value is generated by the observed session population?
- How are these KPIs changing over time?
- Which governed business dimensions contribute to material performance changes?

The layer is intended for monitoring and decision support rather than detailed operational investigation.

Detailed channel, user-behavior, device, and geography analysis remains available through the governed Phase 6 business marts.

---

# 2. Architectural Position

The governed analytical flow is:

```text
GA4 source
    →
staging
    →
intermediate
    →
core warehouse
    →
business marts
    →
executive KPI layer
    →
BI serving layer
    →
Power BI semantic model
    →
Power BI report
```

The Executive KPI Layer consumes governed Phase 6 business marts.

It must not independently reconstruct logic already governed in:

- staging models
- intermediate models
- core warehouse facts or dimensions
- Phase 6 business marts
- Phase 6 KPI contracts

The primary upstream mart for overall executive KPI calculation is:

`mart_ecommerce_daily`

Driver analysis may consume:

- `mart_channel_daily`
- `mart_segment_daily`
- `mart_user_behavior`

only where the analytical question is compatible with the grain and semantic contract of the source mart.

---

# 3. Executive KPI Design Principles

Executive KPIs must:

- answer a defined leadership decision question
- use existing governed KPI definitions
- preserve the approved attribution basis
- preserve the approved reporting-date semantics
- remain reconcilable to Phase 6 business marts
- avoid duplicate business logic
- expose compatible additive components where required for reaggregation
- support controlled trend and comparison calculations
- remain understandable to downstream BI consumers

The Executive KPI Layer must not create alternative definitions of governed Phase 6 KPIs.

# 4. Approved Headline Executive KPI Set

The approved headline KPI set is:

| KPI | Executive Purpose | Semantic Basis | Primary Upstream Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Purchase Revenue | Monitor total governed commercial value | Transaction date | `mart_ecommerce_daily.purchase_revenue` |
| Transaction Count | Monitor governed purchase volume | Transaction date | `mart_ecommerce_daily.transaction_count` |
| Average Order Value | Monitor average commercial value per transaction | Transaction date | `mart_ecommerce_daily.average_order_value` |
| Session Count | Monitor governed traffic volume | Session date | `mart_ecommerce_daily.session_count` |
| Purchasing Session Count | Monitor volume of sessions generating at least one valid purchase | Session date | `mart_ecommerce_daily.purchasing_session_count` |
| Conversion Rate | Monitor session-level purchase efficiency | Session date | `mart_ecommerce_daily.conversion_rate` |
| Revenue per Session | Monitor commercial value generated by the originating session population | Session cohort | `mart_ecommerce_daily.revenue_per_session` |

These KPIs form the controlled headline executive set for Phase 7.

Additional metrics may be used as supporting measures or drivers without automatically becoming headline executive KPIs.

---

# 5. Executive KPI Families

The headline KPI set contains three governed analytical families.

## 5.1 Session-Date KPIs

The following KPIs describe sessions occurring on the reporting date:

- `session_count`
- `purchasing_session_count`
- `conversion_rate`

The reporting date represents:

`session_date`

These metrics answer traffic and session-conversion questions.

## 5.2 Transaction-Date KPIs

The following KPIs describe transaction activity occurring on the reporting date:

- `transaction_count`
- `purchase_revenue`
- `average_order_value`

The reporting date represents:

`transaction_date`

These metrics answer transaction-activity and commercial-performance questions.

## 5.3 Session-Cohort KPIs

The following headline KPI describes commercial outcomes generated by sessions originating on the reporting date:

- `revenue_per_session`

Its numerator is governed session-attributed purchase revenue.

Its denominator is governed session count.

The reporting date represents the originating:

`session_date`

This KPI must remain distinguishable from transaction-date purchase revenue reporting.

---

# 6. Time-Semantic Rule

A shared calendar date in an executive dataset does not imply that every KPI uses the same underlying business event date.

The Executive KPI Layer must preserve the Phase 6 distinction between:

```text
session activity
transaction activity
session-cohort commercial outcomes
```

The layer must not create ratios that combine incompatible metric families.

In particular:

```text
transaction-date purchase_revenue / session-date session_count
```

must not be presented as revenue per session.

Governed `revenue_per_session` must continue to use session-attributed purchase revenue.

Likewise:

```text
transaction-date transaction_count / session-date purchasing_session_count
```

must not be presented as transactions per purchasing session.

Where executive outputs contain multiple metric families on the same calendar row, their semantic family must remain documented and testable.

---

# 7. KPI Ownership

Metric responsibilities are divided across analytical layers.

## Phase 6 Business Marts Own

Phase 6 owns:

- governed base-measure definitions
- governed KPI formulas
- session-date attribution
- transaction-date attribution
- session-cohort attribution
- safe denominator behavior
- governed channel classification
- governed device and geography segmentation
- observed pseudo-user behavioral definitions

Phase 7 must consume these definitions rather than reconstruct them.

## Phase 7 Executive KPI Layer Owns

Phase 7 owns:

- executive KPI selection
- executive reporting structure
- period comparison logic
- trend calculations
- variance calculations
- executive driver calculations
- executive contribution calculations
- executive reconciliation controls
- stable outputs required by the downstream BI serving layer

## Downstream BI Owns

The downstream BI layers may own:

- display formatting
- KPI labels
- visual formatting
- presentation-specific units
- slicer interaction
- visual navigation
- approved presentation calculations that do not redefine governed KPI semantics

BI must not create competing KPI definitions.

---

# 8. Trend Requirements

Phase 7 must support executive interpretation of KPI movement over time.

Trend requirements include controlled calculation of appropriate:

- daily KPI values
- rolling trends where analytically useful
- prior-period values
- absolute variance
- percentage variance

Trend calculations must preserve the semantic family of the underlying KPI.

A transaction-date KPI must be compared with transaction-date history.

A session-date KPI must be compared with session-date history.

A session-cohort KPI must be compared with session-cohort history.

Derived ratios must be recalculated from compatible aggregated numerator and denominator values when the reporting period changes.

Daily ratio values must not simply be averaged to produce period-level KPI values unless an explicit governed metric requires that method.

---

# 9. Driver Requirements

Executive reporting must support controlled explanation of performance changes.

Approved Phase 6 driver sources include:

## Channel Drivers

Source:

`mart_channel_daily`

Potential executive uses:

- traffic contribution by governed channel
- purchasing-session contribution by governed channel
- session-attributed transaction contribution by governed channel
- session-attributed purchase-revenue contribution by governed channel
- conversion-rate comparison by governed channel

Channel commercial measures use session attribution and must not be interpreted as transaction-date activity.

## Device and Geography Drivers

Source:

`mart_segment_daily`

Potential executive uses:

- traffic contribution by device category
- traffic contribution by country
- conversion performance by device category
- conversion performance by country
- session-attributed commercial contribution by device category
- session-attributed commercial contribution by country

Commercial measures in this mart use session attribution.

## Observed User-Behavior Drivers

Source:

`mart_user_behavior`

Potential executive uses:

- purchasing versus non-purchasing pseudo-user populations
- multi-session behavior
- later-date return behavior
- repeat purchasing-session behavior
- repeat purchasing-date behavior
- observed pseudo-user commercial contribution

User-behavior outputs must retain the documented pseudo-user and observation-window limitations.

Driver metrics must not be combined across incompatible grains without an explicitly governed aggregation strategy.

# 10. Supporting Metrics

The following governed metrics may support executive analysis but are not part of the Phase 7 headline KPI set:

- `refund_value`
- `shipping_value`
- `tax_value`
- `total_item_quantity`
- `items_per_transaction`
- `transactions_per_purchasing_session`
- observed pseudo-user population measures
- repeat-behavior population measures

These metrics remain available for supporting analysis where a defined executive question requires them.

Their exclusion from the headline set does not change their governed Phase 6 status.

---

# 11. Explicit Exclusions

The following metrics are not approved for the Phase 7 executive KPI scope unless a future governed design change introduces sufficient source data and business requirements.

## Net Revenue

No governed net-revenue KPI is currently defined.

`refund_value` must not automatically be subtracted from `purchase_revenue`.

## ROAS and Paid-Media Efficiency

Paid-media cost data is unavailable.

Therefore metrics such as:

- ROAS
- cost per acquisition
- customer acquisition cost

cannot be governed from the current dataset.

## Profit and Margin

The current analytical source does not contain sufficient governed cost-of-goods or margin data.

Therefore profit and gross-margin KPIs are outside scope.

## Authenticated Customer KPIs

`user_pseudo_id` does not represent authenticated customer identity.

Therefore the Executive KPI Layer must not introduce unsupported metrics such as:

- true customer count
- new customer count
- returning customer count
- customer retention rate
- customer lifetime value

## Unsupported Attribution Models

The project does not currently implement:

- multi-touch attribution
- paid-media attribution
- alternative marketing attribution models

The governed channel and session attribution established upstream remains authoritative.

---

# 12. Reaggregation Rules

Additive measures may be aggregated across compatible mutually exclusive periods or dimensions when their attribution semantics are preserved.

Derived ratios must be recalculated from their governed additive components.

Examples:

```text
conversion_rate
=
SUM(purchasing_session_count)
/
SUM(session_count)
```

for a compatible multi-day session population.

```text
average_order_value
=
SUM(purchase_revenue)
/
SUM(transaction_count)
```

for a compatible multi-day transaction population.

```text
revenue_per_session
=
SUM(session_attributed_purchase_revenue)
/
SUM(session_count)
```

for a compatible multi-day session cohort.

The Executive KPI Layer must not calculate period-level ratios by averaging daily ratio values.

---

# 13. Reconciliation Requirements

Every executive KPI output must reconcile to the appropriate Phase 6 business mart at an equivalent scope.

Required controls include:

- session measures reconcile to `mart_ecommerce_daily`
- transaction measures reconcile to `mart_ecommerce_daily`
- session-cohort measures reconcile to `mart_ecommerce_daily`
- channel drivers reconcile to `mart_channel_daily`
- device/geography drivers reconcile to `mart_segment_daily`
- observed user-behavior outputs reconcile to `mart_user_behavior`
- period-level derived KPIs reproduce governed formulas from compatible additive components
- trend calculations preserve the correct metric family
- driver calculations do not introduce row multiplication
- no governed business KPI is redefined in Phase 7

Any intentional difference must be documented and approved before implementation is accepted.

# 14. Phase 7 Work-Package Boundaries

## P7A — Executive KPI Scope

Defines:

- executive consumer
- decision use cases
- headline KPI set
- semantic families
- metric ownership
- trend requirements
- driver requirements
- exclusions
- reconciliation expectations

## P7B — KPI Calculation Layer

Implements the governed executive KPI calculation model or models required to expose the approved KPI set and compatible supporting measures.

## P7C — Trend Metrics

Implements approved rolling, comparison, and variance calculations while preserving KPI semantics.

## P7D — Driver Metrics

Implements approved channel, segment, and other executive driver outputs using governed Phase 6 marts.

## P7E — KPI Reconciliation

Validates Phase 7 outputs independently against the governed Phase 6 marts and approved KPI contracts.

## P7F — KPI Documentation

Documents final models, formulas, fields, semantics, limitations, and downstream consumption rules.

---

# 15. P7A Acceptance Criteria

P7A is accepted when:

- the executive consumer and decision use cases are explicit
- the headline KPI set is approved
- each headline KPI has an explicit semantic family
- each headline KPI has an approved Phase 6 source
- session-date, transaction-date, and session-cohort semantics remain distinguishable
- ownership between Phase 6, Phase 7, and downstream BI is explicit
- trend requirements are defined
- driver requirements are defined
- unsupported KPI categories are explicitly excluded
- reaggregation rules are documented
- reconciliation requirements are defined
- no Phase 6 KPI definition is duplicated or contradicted

These scope decisions formed the approved entry criteria for subsequent Phase 7 implementation.

---

# P7A Decision

The approved Phase 7 Executive KPI Layer provides a controlled executive analytical layer over the governed Phase 6 business marts.

The headline KPI set is:

- Purchase Revenue
- Transaction Count
- Average Order Value
- Session Count
- Purchasing Session Count
- Conversion Rate
- Revenue per Session

Phase 7 adds executive reporting structure, trend analysis, period comparison, driver analysis, and reconciliation controls without redefining the governed Phase 6 business metrics.

The distinction between session-date, transaction-date, and session-cohort semantics remains mandatory throughout the Executive KPI Layer.

The approved P7A scope was subsequently implemented through the governed Executive KPI Layer, including:

- `executive_kpi_daily`
- `executive_kpi_trends_daily`

The implemented layer preserves the approved headline KPI set, semantic families, reaggregation rules, driver boundaries, and upstream KPI ownership defined in this scope.

Executive outputs were reconciled against the governed Phase 6 business marts before downstream BI serving and Power BI consumption.
